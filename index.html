Our Story

Four decades of teaching, two chapters of leadership

1986

The school is founded

Raghavan EM High School opens its doors in B. Kotha Kota, Annamayya District, with a mission to bring quality English-medium education to the local community.

2002

New leadership takes over

The school passes into the care of partners S. Venu Madhav (Secretary & Correspondent) and S. Raghu Ram (Academic Director), who begin modernizing classrooms, facilities, and the way the school supports its students.

Today

A growing campus, class by class

From Baby Class through 10th, across A and B sections, the school now combines interactive classrooms, CCTV-monitored safety, and dedicated transport — while holding on to the values it was founded on.

Good to know

Questions parents often ask

Which classes does the school offer?

Every level from Baby Class, LKG and UKG through 1st to 10th Class, with two sections (A and B) in each grade.

What is the medium of instruction and curriculum?

Teaching is in English medium across all levels, with preparation for the State Board of Andhra Pradesh in the higher classes.

Is school transport available?

Yes — dedicated school vehicles serve students from B. Kotha Kota and surrounding areas. Please call the office to ask about your route.

How do I enquire about admission?

Use the Admissions Inquiry Form on this page. The school office will contact you, or you can call or message us on WhatsApp.

How can parents follow their child's progress?

Parents and students can sign in to the school's Parent & Student Portal for attendance, fees, homework and announcements.

Where is the school located?

RSM Road, B. Kotha Kota, Annamayya District, Andhra Pradesh — 517370. See the map in the Contact section.
